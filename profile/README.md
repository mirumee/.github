# **Welcome to the Mirumee Labs**

We are an **Agent-Native Engineering Lab** solving complex challenges in **Commerce** and **MedTech**. We blend years of expertise with AI-driven acceleration to build scalable and compliant systems.

**Open source** is our DNA. As creators and maintainers of global standards like Saleor, Ariadne, and Nimara, we don't just use modern technology – we help define it. 

We love **Python**, **GraphQL**, **TypeScript**, modern frontend frameworks, and doing things the right way.

## **💛 Our Values**

* **Sustainable Open Source** – We build with the long term in mind. Our tools are actively maintained, used in production by global brands, and designed for teams that need reliability, not experiments.  
* **Engineering as the Backbone** – Whether scaling to millions of users or orchestrating complex MedTech architectures, we prioritize performance, security, and scalability from day one.  
* **Agent-Native Acceleration** – We leverage AI agents to amplify our expertise. This allows us to increase velocity and explore edge cases without ever compromising engineering judgment or safety.  
* **Composable & API-First** – We master modular thinking. Every tool we create supports future-proof design, high-performance architecture, and freedom from platform lock-in.  
* **A Team of Experts** – We aren't a factory; we are a Lab. We thrive on long-term partnerships where strategy, radical transparency, and deep engineering move forward together.
